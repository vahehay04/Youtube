Privacy Policy — shorts-agent

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What the app does

shorts-agent is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved to their own channel.

Data we access

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, which allows it to upload videos to the authorized channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data, viewer data, comments, contacts or personal information.

Storage

The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub Actions configuration and is used only to upload videos. No user data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the contact options on the owner's YouTube channel.
