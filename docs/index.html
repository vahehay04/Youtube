shorts-agent

shorts-agent is a private tool used by the owner of a single YouTube channel to upload their own short children's song videos to that channel.

This application uses YouTube API Services.
By using shorts-agent you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. A draft video is prepared and sent to the channel owner for review.
  2. The channel owner manually approves or rejects each draft.
  3. Only approved videos are uploaded to the owner's own channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert, scope youtube.upload), marked as "Made for Kids" and as containing synthetic media.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by the channel owner with their own Google account. It does not read or store any YouTube data, viewer data or comments.
